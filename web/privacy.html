Official Privacy Policy

Bible Study App

Developer / Brand: Waitaminute Digital • Effective Date: September 2026

1 Overview & Commitment

At Waitaminute Digital, we respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how the Bible Study App operates and ensures that your personal information, study habits, and reflections remain strictly confidential and under your control.

Summary: The Bible Study App is designed as an offline-first application. We do not require accounts, do not track your reading, and do not sell or transmit your personal data.

2 Offline-First Architecture & Local Storage

The application is built on an offline-first foundation to guarantee continuous accessibility and privacy:

  • Scripture Text: The full biblical text and canonical references are packaged directly within the local application assets. No network connection is needed to browse, read, or search the scriptures.
  • User Study Notes: All user-authored notes, parsed references, and personal reflections are saved directly onto your device using encrypted, sandboxed local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • Verse Highlights & Bookmarks: Custom color highlights and saved bookmarks reside exclusively on your physical device.
  • Study Questions & Answers: All answers and reflections entered in the study questions module are persisted locally on your device.

Because all data is stored locally, your notes and highlights are never synced to any cloud server operated by us, nor are they accessible to us.

3 Data Collection & Analytics

We uphold a strict zero-data-collection philosophy:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or contact lists.
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing entities.
  • No User Account Requirement: You are never required to log in or create an account to experience all features of the Bible Study App.

4 Advertising, Telemetry & Platform Compliance

The Bible Study App complies with all Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the Google Play Families and Advertising ID policies:

  • Standard underlying platform libraries and runtime components operate in adherence with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable platform advertising identifiers.
  • The app does not link your device advertising ID with any personally identifiable information (PII) or personal study activity.
  • Any diagnostic crash reporting that may occur via standard Google Play Services is managed in accordance with Google's Play Console Developer Terms.

5 Sharing & Exporting Content

When you choose to use the "Share to Chat", "Export PDF", or "Copy Lesson" features, the app uses standard platform sharing sheets (such as Android Intent or iOS UIActivityViewController). Your device controls which third-party application receives the exported lesson or text snippet based solely on your explicit selection.

6 Children's Privacy

The Bible Study App is safe for audiences of all ages and does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this policy to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
